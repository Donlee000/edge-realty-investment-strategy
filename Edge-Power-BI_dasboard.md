# 🏙️ Edge Realty Investment Research

An interactive Power BI dashboard exploring UAE residential and
institutional property investment scenarios for 2026–2030,
using a shared AED 250 million investment ceiling.

---

## Investment Question

How could AED 250 million be allocated across residential properties,
completed institutional facilities and institutional development,
while comparing rental income, capital commitments and unspent cash?

## Dashboard Coverage

### 01 — Residential Opportunities

- Rental yield comparisons by location and bedroom type
- Acquisition cost, annual gross rent and selected units
- Single-location versus multiple-location portfolio comparisons
- Capital deployed and cash remaining

### 02 — Institutional Strategies

- Buying completed facilities versus developing new facilities
- Annual net rental income from 2026 to 2030
- Portfolio comparisons focused on net rent and total profit
- Delivery schedules, estimated capital exposure and missing exit valuations

## Tools Used

Power BI · DAX · Power Query · Deneb · Python · SQL · R

## About the Data

This independent case study uses synthetic property scenarios.
Illustrative visuals and estimated capital schedules are identified
within the report.

Residential annual gross rent and institutional net rental income
use different income bases and should be interpreted accordingly.
